The ALU design uses a total area of 11563.55 square micrometers and consists of 5175 combinational cells and no sequential cells (which is correct, 
because it's combinational). 
This large area is due to the multiplication operator, which requires a significant number of logic gates compared 
to simpler bitwise functions. The timing report shows that the multiplier also defines the critical path of the circuit. Because carry 
signals must propagate through many logic levels during multiplication, this operation determines the maximum delay. 
In contrast, logical shifts and bitwise operations are much faster as they involve simpler wiring.
