Question 1
A standard N-bit addition in VHDL does not provide the N+1 bit required for the Carry-Out so, to fix the behavioral RCA, we must extend the input 
operands by one bit (using an additional '0' at the MSB) before the addition.

Question 2
The difference between S1, S2, and S3 is the timing of the output: S1 is the behavioral implementation and provides an ideal result with zero 
delay. S2 and S3 are structural implementations with specific delays (DRCAS and DRCAC), so they reach the correct value later than S1.

Question 3
The outputs S2 and S3 glitch, S1 doesn't. S2 and S3 have the delay of the carry. This happens because the carry signal must "ripple" 
through each FA stage in the structural chain.
